Frequently Asked Questions

How do I purchase your products?

All our products are available on our Etsy store. Simply visit ArtifyCreativeHub on Etsy to browse and purchase.

What formats are the files in?

Most of our products are delivered as high-resolution PDF files, perfect for printing. Some products may include additional formats like PNG or JPEG. Check individual product descriptions for specific format details.

Do you offer commercial licenses?

Yes! Many of our KDP bundles include commercial use rights. Please check the individual product listing for specific licensing information.

Can I request custom designs?

We occasionally take custom orders. Please contact us through Etsy messages to discuss your specific needs and we'll let you know if we can accommodate your request.

How do I download my purchase?

After purchase, you'll receive an email from Etsy with a download link. You can also access your files through your Etsy account under "Purchases and Reviews."

What if I have issues with my download?

If you experience any problems with your download or the files, please contact us immediately through Etsy messages and we'll resolve the issue promptly.

Still Have Questions?

Contact us through our Etsy store and we'll be happy to help!
